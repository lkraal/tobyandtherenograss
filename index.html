About the band

Toby and the Reno Grass plays an exciting mix of traditional and progressive bluegrass in the basin and range. Fiddle tunes, three-part harmonies, and the occasional song you didn't expect to hear on a banjo.

Based in Reno, Nevada, the band plays breweries, festivals, farmers markets, weddings and private events across the Reno/Tahoe area and the Sierra foothills.
